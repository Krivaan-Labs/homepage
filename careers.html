How We Hire

Simple, Respectful Process

Step 1
Application Review

We read every application carefully. You'll hear back within 5 business days — no black holes.

Step 2
Intro Call (30 min)

A relaxed conversation with someone from the team to learn about each other. No trick questions.

Step 3
Technical Exercise

A focused take-home exercise (max 3 hours) that mirrors real work, followed by a live review discussion.

Step 4
Final Interview

Meet the founder and one other team member. We'll dive deeper into your experience and answer all your questions.

Step 5
Offer

If it's a mutual fit, we move fast. Offer letters typically go out within 24 hours of the final interview.
