FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you start a project?

For most projects we can begin within 1–2 weeks. Enterprise engagements with procurement requirements may take a bit longer for contracting.

Do you offer fixed-price or time-and-materials engagements?

Both. For well-defined scopes we prefer fixed-price milestones with clear deliverables. For exploratory or ongoing work, we use time-and-materials with weekly reporting.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely. We're happy to sign mutual NDAs before any detailed discussions about your project. Just mention it in your message.

What is your minimum engagement size?

We typically work best on projects with at least a 4-week engagement. For smaller needs like architecture reviews or code audits, we offer fixed-scope packages.
